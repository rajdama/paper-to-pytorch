repo step 03–05 · paper §3.2

Attention

softmax(QKᵀ / √dk) V

Each row is a token deciding how much to look at every other token. Rows always sum to 1.

Head
avg. strongest weight – dk 16
0 1

Weights here are untrained and random, so patterns don't mean anything yet. What's real is the mechanics: turn off √dk scaling and raise the magnitude, and rows collapse onto one token (softmax saturation). Turn on the causal mask and the upper triangle disappears.

repo step 02 · paper §3.5

Positional encoding

PE(pos, 2i) = sin(pos / 100002i/d)

Every position gets a unique pattern of sine and cosine waves. Early dimensions oscillate fast, later ones slowly, like the hands of a clock.

repo step 11 · paper §5.3

Learning-rate schedule

d−0.5 · min(step−0.5, step · warmup−1.5)

Rise linearly during warmup, then decay with the inverse square root of the step. The paper uses dmodel = 512 and 4000 warmup steps.
